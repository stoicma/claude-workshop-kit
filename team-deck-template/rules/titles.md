# Action title rules

## The rule

Every slide title is a complete sentence stating a conclusion. Not a topic. Not a label. A claim the slide proves.

## Wrong vs right

| Wrong (topic title) | Right (action title) |
|---|---|
| Market Overview | The EV market in the Western Balkans grew 47% in 2025 |
| Competitive Landscape | Three local incumbents control 80% of charging infrastructure |
| Our Recommendation | We recommend entry via a JV with the Serbian utility |
| Risk Analysis | Regulatory risk is concentrated in two jurisdictions |
| Customer Segmentation | Premium buyers in Croatia and Slovenia drive 60% of revenue |
| Financial Model | A 3-year ramp returns capital by Year 4 in the base case |
| Timeline | Year 1 focuses on Serbia; expansion follows in Year 2 |

The wrong column tells the reader what is on the slide. The right column tells the reader what to think after seeing the slide.

## Constraints

- Maximum **12 words**. If you need more, you have two messages and should split the slide.
- **Verb-led when possible.** "We recommend X" beats "X is our recommendation."
- **Numbers when defensible.** "Revenue grew 47%" beats "Revenue grew significantly."
- **No vague hedging.** "May potentially impact" → "reduces by 20%".
- **No question marks** as titles. Questions are bodies, not conclusions.
- **No colons used to dodge the rule.** "Market Overview: The EV Market Grew 47%" is still a topic title with a sentence stapled on.

## Patterns that work

- **The bottom line.** "X delivers Y by Z."
- **The comparison.** "Option A returns 2× Option B over 5 years."
- **The threshold.** "Below 10,000 units we lose money in every scenario."
- **The mechanism.** "Regulatory delays add 18 months to the base case."
- **The asymmetry.** "Three of seven competitors capture 80% of the margin."

## Voice

Marko's house rules apply when these decks are produced for stokic.ai or workshop attendees:
- No em dashes anywhere. Use periods plus short next sentences.
- No AI-tell phrases. Avoid "leverage" as a verb, "delve into", "navigate complexities", "in today's fast-paced".
- No triple-adjective clusters ("robust scalable resilient platform").

## Title-only sanity check

Read the titles of the deck in order. They should form a paragraph. If a title is missing or weak, the next one will not flow. Tighten until reading the titles end-to-end produces a coherent argument.
